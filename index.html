Getting Started

1
Browse the tree - tap any dark header bar to expand that generation and see the family.
2
Tap any person (colored oval with their name) to open their full profile at the bottom of the screen.
3
Add a story - in the profile panel, type a memory or fun fact in the Stories box and tap Save Story.
4
Add a person - tap the gold "+ Add Person" button, fill in what you know, pick their role, and the color applies automatically.
5
Add a child - expand any generation and tap "+ add child to this family" under any couple.
6
Search - use the search bar at the top to find any name instantly.
